02 · About

Edits built around the story

Every cut lands on the voiceover. B-roll matches the exact line it sits under, captions are readable on a phone with the sound off, and the music sits under the voice, never on top of it.

I source licensed stock and public-domain archive footage myself, so you never get a copyright claim, and every video ships with a list of credits.
